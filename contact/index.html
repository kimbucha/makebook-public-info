Contact Makebook

For help with your account, a book, or an export, get in touch with Makebook Support.

Email Makebook Support

makebooksupport@gmail.com

Use the same address for privacy questions and requests about your information. The privacy notice explains how Makebook handles saved work, uploads, and support messages.

What to include

  • A short description of what you were trying to do and what happened.
  • The book title and approximate time of the problem, if relevant.
  • Any error message, with private information removed.

Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes, API keys, or a complete manuscript. Start with a description; if more information is needed, we can discuss it by email.
